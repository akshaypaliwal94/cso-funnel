### 1 · DIAGNOSIS
**Eyebrow:** THE NUMBERS NOBODY EVER SHOWED YOU
**Badge:** EVERY LEAK, IN RUPEES · WEEK 1-2

- **A proper session with you first** — what you think is broken, and what you have already tried to fix

### 2 · CLOSING
**Eyebrow:** SELLING WITHOUT SOUNDING SALESY
**Badge:** MORE YES, AT A HIGHER PRICE · WEEK 3-4

- **A pitch built around how much they already know** — unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, or ready to buy. Four different conversations, not one script.

## FAQs

**How is this different from a sales coach or a course?**

A course is an event. You watch it, you feel motivated, and three weeks later nothing has actually changed in your business.

This is an install. I build the pitch, the objection answers and the follow-up systems inside your business, then stay on to listen to your real calls every month and tell you what to fix.

**I close everything myself. I don't have a sales team. Does this still work?**

Yes — and it is often easier.

Nothing here needs a team. Your pitch gets built from your own best calls, not from a template. The AI systems do the follow-up you are currently doing at 11pm, or not doing at all.

The only thing I need is enough activity to diagnose properly.

**3X in 60 days sounds too good. Is it realistic?**

It is not one big jump. It is three small ones stacking on top of each other.

Say 100 leads come in this month. Ten of them buy, at ₹1 lakh each. That is ₹10 lakh.

Now three things each improve by up to 50%:

- 15 people buy instead of 10
- Each one pays ₹1.5 lakh instead of ₹1 lakh
- Your follow-up rescues another 7 deals you were writing off completely

The same 100 leads can make you over ₹30 lakh.

Nothing doubled. Nothing tripled. Three separate numbers went up by 50% — and because they multiply instead of adding up, you land past 3X.

**What exactly is included?**

It's a completely done-for-you service with 1:1 consulting. It includes everything in the four phases — the diagnosis, the pitch and objection playbook, the design of all seven AI systems, the dashboard, and the weekly review.

**How much of my time will this take?**

Weeks 1 and 2 need real time from you — one proper working session, plus access to your recordings and numbers. After that it is a 1-hr weekly call.

The whole point is to take sales off your plate, not to add one more thing you have to manage. If I need three hours of your week for sixty days, I have built it wrong.
